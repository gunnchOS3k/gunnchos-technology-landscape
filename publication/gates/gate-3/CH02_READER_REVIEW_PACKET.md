Broader multi-cohort field validation remains **Gate 5**. This packet supports the Gate 3 requirement of testing with at least three reader levels.

## How to use

1. Give the reader Chapter 2 preview (`make preview` → `preview/ch02.html`) or PDF.
2. Ask them to complete Route A or Route B of LAB-TAP-001 (or the supplied fixture route).
3. Collect one form per reader.
4. Store completed forms outside fabricated evidence paths until real humans respond.
