## Explicit prohibition: KDP Select under this objective

KDP Select enrollment makes the **digital** format exclusive to KDP for the exclusivity
period (cannot distribute digitally elsewhere, including the author website). That
conflicts with Apple / Google / Kobo / direct free access.

**Decision for this track:** Do **not** enroll adult Kindle title in KDP Select while
the wide-access objective remains in force.

Sources: `https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/select` (exclusivity language retrieved 2026-09-03);
`https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G200798990`.
