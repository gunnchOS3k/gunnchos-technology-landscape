Electricity, Signals, Clocks, and Logic

## Primary reader promise

After this chapter, a reader can explain how everyday device behavior rests on electricity, signals over time, shared clocks, and logic decisions—connecting human moments to physical fundamentals without cataloguing every component.

## Anchor human moment

You press a button or touch a screen. Nothing ‘magical’ happens in the glass alone: voltages change, a signal is recognized as a discrete event, a clock keeps order, and logic decides what the system should do next.
