## Canonical title

Power, Batteries, Thermals, and Mechanical Design

## Primary reader promise

After this chapter, a reader can explain why power, batteries, heat, and mechanical design shape what technology can sustainably feel like—and why ‘performance’ collapses when energy/thermal budgets tighten.

## Anchor human moment

On battery in a warm room, the same app that felt fine on a charger now hitchs, dims, or throttles. The software did not ‘forget how’; the energy and thermal budgets changed.
