## Canonical title

Wi-Fi, Cellular, 5G, and the Road to 6G

## Primary reader promise

After this chapter, a reader can distinguish Wi-Fi vs cellular as different access technologies, place 5G in a standards evolution story, and treat 6G as a roadmap—not a present synonym for better bars.

## Experience-first opening moment (section intent)

**Canonical anchor:** Leaving a building, Wi-Fi drops and cellular takes over (or the reverse); a 5G icon appears while a call still feels bad. From the seat: I have 5G so it should be fine. Underneath: different RATs, operators, and core networks.

## Part emphasis

Access-network literacy; do not collapse into Internet/cloud; 6G = road map humility.

- Backhaul/core path usable
