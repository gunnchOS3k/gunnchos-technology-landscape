## Stability Contract (chapter conditions)

- Input data quality / availability within acceptable bounds
- Model/runtime availability and version consistency
- Latency/energy budget for interactive answers
- Disclosure and logging posture remain usable and honest
- Uncertainty remains visible enough that fluent errors are catchable
