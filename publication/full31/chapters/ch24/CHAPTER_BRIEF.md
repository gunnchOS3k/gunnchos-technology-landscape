## Stability Contract (chapter conditions)

- Consent/disclosure understandable and actionable
- Identity recovery usable without excluding assistive paths
- Data lifecycle controls (retain/delete/redact) operable
- Accessibility path remains concurrent with other success conditions
- Safety constraints prevent harmful fluent outputs from being actionable blindly
