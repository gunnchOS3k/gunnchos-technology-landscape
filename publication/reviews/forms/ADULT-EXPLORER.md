**Role:** Nontechnical / general reader. Critical feedback is desired. You are not asked to praise the work. You may decline or stop at any time.

## Mode
Whole-book navigation review: front matter, selected anchor chapters, one chapter per Part, glossary/navigation, overall progression.

## Primary questions
1. Could you follow the idea?
2. Where did you get lost?
3. Which figure helped?
4. Which terms needed clearer introduction?
5. Did the chapter connect technology to lived experience?
