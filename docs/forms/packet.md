**Time:** about **75–120 minutes** total (see `REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md`)

## How to use

1. Open the reader landing page (`docs/index.html` on GitHub Pages, or the `reader-preview-bundle` artifact).
2. Read Chapter 2 (HTML/PDF/EPUB) and inspect figures — cite snapshot **CH02-REVIEW-R1**.
3. Complete LAB-TAP-001 **browser** route or **fixture** route.
4. Fill **one** form for your reader level (A–D).
5. Return the form to the maintainer; structured YAML may be filed under `responses/` after redaction.

Privacy: use a **reviewer code / pseudonym**; name is optional. Do not share passwords, tokens, private messages, device serials, or precise location.

Machine schema: `reader_feedback_schema.yaml`
