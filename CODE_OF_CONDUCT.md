# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a harassment-free learning and contribution environment.

## Expected

- Respect learners at every pathway level
- Credit contributors by role
- Prefer evidence over prestige language
- Protect privacy in screenshots, logs, and examples

## Unacceptable

- Harassment, discrimination, or contempt toward beginners
- Sharing secrets, tokens, private identifiers, or personal data
- Fabricating evidence or pressuring others to overclaim

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the author/maintainers. Sustained violations may result in removal from project spaces.
