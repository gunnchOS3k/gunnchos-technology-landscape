## Spread S01 — Two taps, two stories (OBSERVE)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S01](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S01.svg)

**Child-facing text:** Mira and Bolt sit with a tablet. First, Mira taps a lamp icon that only changes this device — the screen brightens right away. Next, Mira taps Refresh on a class page that sometimes needs the network. Watch both taps carefully. Write or draw: What changed fast? What waited? Keep your notes for the portfolio page later.

**Try it:** Observe one local tap and one network-ish tap; record what was seen.

**Talk together:** Supervise. No accounts. Separate “I saw” from “I think.”

## Spread S02 — Hardware and software (NAME)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S02](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S02.svg)

**Child-facing text:** Bolt points under the glass: hardware senses the touch — the touchscreen and chips you can touch if a grown-up opened the case. Step holds a checklist: software is the instructions that decide what happens next. Hardware notices. Software chooses. Say both words and point to an example.

**Try it:** Name hardware vs software with one example each.

**Talk together:** Keep definitions short and pointed at the device.

## Spread S03 — Input → process → output (NAME)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S03](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S03.svg)

**Child-facing text:** Fill three boxes with Mira. Input: the touch report (“someone pressed here”). Process: the app decides what to do. Output: pixels, sound, or motion you can notice. Draw arrows Input → Process → Output for the lamp tap.

**Try it:** Complete an IPO diagram for one local tap.

**Talk together:** IPO diagram on paper or whiteboard.

## Spread S04 — A four-step algorithm (PREDICT)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S04](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S04.svg)

**Child-facing text:** Step asks for a four-step algorithm for the local lamp tap. Example shape: 1) sense press 2) check which control 3) change brightness 4) show new light. Write your own clear order. Algorithms are recipes — order matters.

**Try it:** Write a 4-step algorithm for a local tap.

**Talk together:** Any clear order is OK; fix together if stuck.

## Spread S05 — Local vs network (TEST)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S05](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S05.svg)

**Child-facing text:** Test time. Which tap stayed local — finished on this device? Which tap waited as if a message went out and came back? Ping draws two paths: a short loop on the tablet, and a longer path that leaves the room. Classify your two taps. Mark guesses with a question mark.

**Try it:** Classify taps as local vs network; mark uncertain inferences.

**Talk together:** Observation vs guess — label both.

## Spread S06 — Why feedback felt instant (EXPLAIN)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S06](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S06.svg)

**Child-facing text:** Sometimes a button highlights instantly even when new words are still traveling. Explain two timelines: Timeline A — local highlight. Timeline B — remote content. Instant does not always mean “finished everywhere.”

**Try it:** Explain two timelines for one networked-feeling tap.

**Talk together:** Two timelines on one page.

## Spread S07 — Safe practice (SECURE)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S07](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S07.svg)

**Child-facing text:** Shield’s checklist: Do not install unknown apps. Ask before sharing your name, photo, or school. Locks and passwords are tools, not toys to share. Safety without scare stories — we practice calm choices.

**Try it:** Complete a short safety checklist aloud or on paper.

**Talk together:** Safety without fear.

## Spread S08 — Observation worksheet (BUILD)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S08](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S08.svg)

**Child-facing text:** Build a portfolio page with three columns: What I saw · What I infer · What I still don’t know. Use your lamp tap and refresh tap. Inferences stay uncertain until tested. Unknowns are honest science.

**Try it:** Complete observation worksheet for portfolio.

**Talk together:** Portfolio piece — process, not ranking.

## Spread S09 — Misconception check (REFLECT)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S09](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S09.svg)

**Child-facing text:** True or rethink: “Every tap goes to the internet.” Discuss kindly with Mira. Many taps finish locally. Some need a network. Evidence from your tests beats the slogan.

**Try it:** Revisit the misconception with evidence.

**Talk together:** Discuss kindly; no shame.

## Spread S10 — Teach-back IPO (TEACH)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S10](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-ELEM1-S10.svg)

**Child-facing text:** Teach a partner or caregiver the IPO path for one tap. Use Mira’s cast roles if it helps: Bolt for parts you can touch, Step for instructions, Ping for optional messages, Shield for pause-and-check. Stop anytime. This prototype is for learning practice — not a test score.

**Try it:** Teach-back IPO path.

**Talk together:** Optional peer; easy stop.
