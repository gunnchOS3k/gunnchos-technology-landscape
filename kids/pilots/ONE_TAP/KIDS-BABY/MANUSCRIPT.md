## Caregiver / educator note

Follow gaze. Soft voice.

## Spread S01 — Look (LOOK)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S01](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S01.svg)

**Child-facing text:** Look.

**Action:** Look together at a single lit control.

**Caregiver prompt:** Follow gaze. Soft voice.

## Spread S02 — Hand (POINT)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S02](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S02.svg)

**Child-facing text:** Hand.

**Action:** Notice hand near the surface.

**Caregiver prompt:** Point to hand, then control.

## Spread S03 — Touch (NAME)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S03](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S03.svg)

**Child-facing text:** Touch.

**Action:** Finger meets surface.

**Caregiver prompt:** Name the touch when contact happens.

## Spread S04 — Wait (WAIT)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S04](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S04.svg)

**Child-facing text:** Wait…

**Action:** A short wait before change.

**Caregiver prompt:** Pause. Do not rush.

## Spread S05 — Change (RESPOND)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S05](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S05.svg)

**Child-facing text:** Change!

**Action:** Light or sound changes.

**Caregiver prompt:** Celebrate the change calmly.

## Spread S06 — Again (REPEAT)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S06](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S06.svg)

**Child-facing text:** Again?

**Action:** Optional second touch.

**Caregiver prompt:** Offer repeat; accept no.

## Spread S07 — Same place (NAME)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S07](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S07.svg)

**Child-facing text:** Same.

**Action:** Repeat at same control.

**Caregiver prompt:** Same spot → same kind of change.

## Spread S08 — New place (LOOK)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S08](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S08.svg)

**Child-facing text:** New.

**Action:** Move attention to another control.

**Caregiver prompt:** Different control, different change.

## Spread S09 — Point with me (POINT)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S09](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S09.svg)

**Child-facing text:** Point.

**Action:** Point to what changed.

**Caregiver prompt:** Invite pointing; no demand for speech.

## Spread S10 — All done (RESPOND)

![FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S10](figures/FIG-ONE-TAP-BABY-S10.svg)

**Child-facing text:** All done.

**Action:** Session ends positively.

**Caregiver prompt:** Easy stop. Close the book/device.
