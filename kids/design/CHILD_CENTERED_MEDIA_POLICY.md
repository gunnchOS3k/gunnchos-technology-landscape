# Child-Centered Media Policy

**Status:** `DRAFT_INTERNAL`  
**Evidence basis:** `kids/research/CHILD_MEDIA_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.yaml` (AAP, WHO, ICO AADC, COPPA, UNICEF, serve-and-return, JME)  
**Non-claim:** Policy draft for production; not a legal opinion; not child-validated.

## Purpose

Learning and wellbeing over maximum engagement. The book and any digital companion may be captivating without being compulsive.

## Non-negotiables

1. **Learning/wellbeing over engagement metrics** — optimize comprehension, transfer, sleep, and play — not time-on-device.
2. **No infinite scroll / auto-queue** — each unit has a clear end.
3. **Autoplay off by default** — user/caregiver starts media deliberately.
4. **No manipulative variable rewards** — no loot-box timing, streak shame, or intermittent reward traps.
5. **No targeted advertising to children.**
6. **No dark patterns** — no disguised ads, fake urgency, or hard-to-find exit.
7. **No unnecessary data collection** — offline-first; minimize PII; no child profiling for ads.
8. **No chat with strangers.**
9. **No pressure loops** — no “don’t leave now” guilt, countdown coercion, or forced continuous play.
10. **Easy stop/exit points** — every digital surface has an obvious stop.
11. **Offline activity encouraged** — each unit points to a hands-on / real-world action.
12. **Caregiver co-use encouraged for younger ages** (especially under ~5–6).
13. **Print-first for infants/toddlers** — digital is optional companion, never the sole path to core learning.

## Age posture

| Band | Media posture |
| --- | --- |
| KIDS-BABY | Print board book + caregiver; optional short audio; no solo interactive digital product |
| KIDS-TODDLER | Print-first; co-use only if digital exists |
| KIDS-PRESCHOOL / PREK | Print + light digital with wait prompts; co-use default |
| KIDS-ELEM1 / ELEM2 | Print/digital balanced; agency and privacy explicit; still no compulsion patterns |

## Prohibited claim language

Do not ship copy that asserts unsupported sensory-neuroscience (exact color activates brain, 432 Hz intelligence, high pitch makes children learn, etc.).
