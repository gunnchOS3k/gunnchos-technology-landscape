# Kids Audio / Prosody Guide

**Status:** `DRAFT_INTERNAL`  
**Evidence:** CME-008–012, CME-014, CME-018–021  
**Hard rule:** No mandatory audio for access to core learning. Captions/transcript required for any shipped digital audio/video.

**Do not produce final audio assets** in this wave without voice licensing and production requirements.

## Goals

Optional read-aloud / audio companion that models natural expressive caregiver speech — not cartoon squeak, not ASMR gimmick, not frequency pseudoscience.

## Global rules

- Natural expressive speech with clear enunciation.
- Pitch **variation** and warm affect — not constant high pitch.
- No unsupported frequency claims (including 432 Hz intelligence myths).
- No startling peaks; soft limiters; avoid sudden loud SFX.
- Sound-effect restraint: rare, meaningful, never masking speech.
- Call-and-response only with explicit silence for child/caregiver response.
- Caregiver pauses written into scripts (`[WAIT 2–4 s]` style ranges — not lab-precise mandates).
- Repetition of key labels with slight contextual variation.

## Age-band rate guidance (qualitative)

| Band | Rate / delivery | Pause guidance | Emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BABY | Slow, very clear; short phrases | Long waits after naming | Soft; name object child looks at |
| TODDLER | Slow–moderate | Wait after questions / points | Stress new word once, repeat later |
| PRESCHOOL | Moderate CDS | 2–4 s after prompts (range) | Contrast old vs new idea |
| PREK | Moderate, slightly more adult | 2–3 s | Concept words + why |
| ELEM1 | Near-natural clear speech | Short think-time | Technical term + plain gloss |
| ELEM2 | Natural speech | Think-time for predict/test | Precise terms; avoid baby voice |

Exact words-per-minute targets are **not** mandated without measurement studies; treat the table as production direction.

## Scripts and IP

Study participation/repetition methodology from research; write **original** lines. Do not imitate copyrighted songs, catchphrases, or character voices from living shows.

## Rejected audio claims

- “High pitch makes children learn”
- “This Hz improves intelligence / calm / focus” as product fact
- Continuous background music that competes with speech for under-5s
