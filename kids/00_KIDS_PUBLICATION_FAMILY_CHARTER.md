# Kids Publication Family Charter

**Status:** `DRAFT_INTERNAL`  
**Track:** Kids Development + Media + Design (K1, K8, K9)  
**Wave:** publication-family parallel production  
**Non-claim:** This charter is not publication-ready evidence and does not imply Gate advancement.

## Core promise

> Give children a developmentally appropriate understanding of how technology works, from cause/effect and systems thinking through genuine computing, networking, AI, security, engineering, and societal responsibility.

## Non-negotiable

The goal is **advantage through deep understanding**, not premature memorization.

Do **not** frame a toddler as failing because they cannot perform an older-child outcome. Ages are guides, not hard developmental deadlines.

## Evidence and honesty

- Primary evidence: peer-reviewed research and professional developmental / pediatric bodies (`kids/research/`).
- Study children's-media **methodology** (repetition, pacing, prosody, participation). Do **not** imitate copyrighted characters, scripts, songs, or plots.
- Prohibit unsupported sensory-neuroscience claims (exact-color brain activation, 432 Hz intelligence, “high pitch makes children learn,” etc.).
- No child testing, recruitment, or child data collection in this wave.

## Media ethics summary

Learning and wellbeing over maximum engagement. Print-first for infants/toddlers. Caregiver co-use for younger ages. No infinite scroll, autoplay-by-default, dark patterns, targeted ads to children, stranger chat, or unnecessary data collection. Full policy: `kids/design/CHILD_CENTERED_MEDIA_POLICY.md`.
