# Accessibility Notes — KIDS-BABY


Adult-facing notes for mediating the board book. Not child-facing prose.

## Principles

- **Print-first** high-contrast spreads (~800×500 diagrams) with empty board real-estate.
- **Color is never the sole encoding** — shape, position, and caregiver labels reinforce meaning (CME-003, CME-004).
- **No speech required.** Gaze, reach, stilling, smiling, or calm attending all count.
- **One focal action** per spread (CME-001, CME-005).
- **Easy stop** honored immediately (CME-022–024).

## Routes by need

| Need | Route |
| --- | --- |
| Low vision | Larger print caregiver cards; tactile props; sound-on-change |
| Blind / dual sensory | Fully tactile household cause→effect with verbal naming |
| Deaf / hard of hearing | Visual change emphasis; vibration toys; signed labels by caregiver |
| Motor difference | Caregiver performs action; baby participates via gaze/switch if used |
| Attention variability | Shorter sessions; single spread loops; reduce sound |

## What we do not do

- No diagnostic or screening claims (CME-028, CME-029).
- No mandatory audio track.
- No autoplay or dark-pattern re-engagement (CME-025–027).
- No inaccessible color-only diagrams.

## Figure a11y

All shipped SVGs include `<title>` and `<desc>`, `role="img"`, and print-safe dimensions. Narrative art remains `ILLUSTRATION_DIRECTION_READY` until commissioned with equivalent alt text.
