## Role

You are the primary learning partner. The book supports **serve-and-return** with technology precursors — not solo “educational screen time.”

## Session shape

1. Choose one unit (or even one spread).
2. Follow gaze (LOOK).
3. Point gently (POINT).
4. Name with few words (NAME).
5. Pause (WAIT).
6. Notice any change or comfort (RESPOND).
7. Offer once more or stop (REPEAT / easy stop).

## Do / Don’t

**Do:** print or hold still pages; use household objects; celebrate calmly; stop early.  
**Don’t:** quiz; force words; collect data; open child accounts; run messaging/AI demos; claim standards certification.

## Observation (not assessment)

Optional private notes: which spread brought delight? what cue did you notice? Never scoring, never milestone diagnosis.
