## Rules
- Stage 1 adult reviews may use PUBLIC_SAFE sanitized findings.
- Stage 2 child session notes: no child names, school names, addresses, emails, phones, photos, or recordings in the public repo.
- Validators reject obvious email/phone/address patterns in child response artifacts.
- Guardian contact information is PRIVATE_REVIEW_DATA / out of repo.
