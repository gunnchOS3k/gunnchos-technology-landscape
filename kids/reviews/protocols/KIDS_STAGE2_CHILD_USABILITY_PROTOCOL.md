## Prerequisites before any child session
1. Stage 1 findings triaged
2. BLOCKER/MAJOR developmental issues fixed
3. Owner decides provisional character/cast direction
4. Review prototypes regenerated
5. Guardian-facing consent/permission materials ready
6. Owner completes `kids/review-candidates/KIDS_STAGE2_OWNER_DECISIONS.md`

## Child session principles
- guardian permission
- adult supervision
- child assent where developmentally meaningful
- easy stop at any time
- no account creation
- no unnecessary PII
- no addresses / email / phone in repo
- no advertising
- no hidden analytics
- no hidden recording
- no diagnostic claims
- no ranking children
- no pressure to finish
- no rewards designed to coerce continuation

## Age-specific observation
### Infant/toddler
Caregiver-observed interaction: looking, pointing, reaching, affect, disengagement. No verbal performance requirement.

### Preschool/Pre-K
Story/interaction usability; pointing, sequence, prediction, simple teach-back if naturally appropriate. No exam behavior.

### Elementary
Comprehension-in-context; activity usability; explanation; misconception detection; navigation; engagement without compulsion.
