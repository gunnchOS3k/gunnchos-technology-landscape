# Caregiver / Educator Notes — KIDS-PREK

**Audience:** adults only · NOT CHILD-VALIDATED · NOT PUBLICATION-READY

## How to facilitate

1. Follow attention; name; wait; never force performance.
2. Introduce 1–2 precise terms with pointing.
3. Mark predictions as guesses.
4. Celebrate repair when sequences break.
5. Keep privacy talk calm — choose safer paths without scary stories.
6. Stop anytime; observation notes only.
