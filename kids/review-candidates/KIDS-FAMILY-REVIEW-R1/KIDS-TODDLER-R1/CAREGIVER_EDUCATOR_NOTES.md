## Session rhythm

1. **LOOK** — follow the child's attention.
2. **POINT** — invite a point (finger, gaze, AAC).
3. **NAME** — one short phrase.
4. **WAIT** — pause; do not rush.
5. **RESPOND** — name the change.
6. **REPEAT** — once with joy; stop anytime.

## Co-use rules

- Print-first. Optional digital only beside a caregiver.
- Supervise every device touch.
- No stranger chat, no child accounts, no data collection.
- Observation notes only — never score or screen the child.
