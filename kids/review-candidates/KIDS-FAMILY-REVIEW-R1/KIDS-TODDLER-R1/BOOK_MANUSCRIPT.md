## How to use

1. Sit together. Follow the child's eyes.
2. One short phrase. Then wait.
3. Point, match, or try once — stop anytime.
4. Repeat with joy; never force a performance.

## Caregiver orientation

You are the co-reader and play partner. Name what you see. Connect book objects to safe home switches and buttons. Home language welcome. Observation only — no quizzes, no scores.

## Provisional cast (Option A — Signal Crew)

| Role | Name | Toddler presence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explorer | Mira | Points and wonders |
| Builder | Bolt | Things you can touch |
| Instructions | Step | First, then next |
| Signals | Ping | Here → there |
| Safety | Shield | Pause and ask |

Characters are gentle presence motifs, not required on every spread. Silhouettes stay provisional until owner IP lock.

## Safety & accessibility (session)

- Supervise all device touch.
- No stranger chat. No child accounts. No data collection.
- Easy stop. Autoplay off.
- Accept point, gesture, AAC, or vocalize.
- Color is never the only cue.
