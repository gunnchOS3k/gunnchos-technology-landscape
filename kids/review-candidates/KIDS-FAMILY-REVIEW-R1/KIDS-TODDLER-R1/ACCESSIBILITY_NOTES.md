## Band defaults

- High contrast diagrams; large type targets; 1–2 focals per spread.
- Color never sole encoding — labels/shapes/order carry meaning.
- Print stands alone; audio optional caregiver read-aloud only.
- Motor: large point targets; no precision-only gestures; block play OK with help.
- Neurodiversity: predictable LOOK→POINT→NAME→WAIT→RESPOND→REPEAT; easy stop; no surprise SFX claims.
- AAC / multimodal: accept point, gesture, device, or vocalize; never require spoken answers.
- Multilingual: caregiver may translate phrases; no fake validated translations.

## Figure a11y

Every deterministic SVG includes `title`, `desc`, `role="img"`, and reading order title → focal → body. Narrative art remains `ILLUSTRATION_DIRECTION_READY` until commissioned.

## Non-claims

Accessibility notes are a checklist — not WCAG/EPUB certification.

## Route checklist (integrator completeness)

- Low vision routes: large print, high contrast, described figures
- Color vision: redundant non-color cues
- Hearing: visual alternatives; no audio-only requirements
- Dyslexia / readability: short lines, clear spacing
- Motor differences: large targets, partner-assisted options
- Neurodiversity: predictable cadence, easy stop, low clutter
- AAC / communication differences: gesture/device/partner responses count
- Sensory sensitivity: quiet route available
- Multilingual families: caregiver translation welcome
