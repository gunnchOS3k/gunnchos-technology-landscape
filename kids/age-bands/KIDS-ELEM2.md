# KIDS-ELEM2 Age-Band Architecture

**Band ID:** `KIDS-ELEM2`  
**Guide ages:** Grades 3–5/6 (guide, not deadline)  
**Status:** `DRAFT_INTERNAL`

## Learner profile

Capable of layered systems, measurement, critique of unfair tech, and portfolio evidence. Still needs clarity and anti-overwhelm layout.

## Caregiver role

Supportive; educator-led pathways common.

## Language complexity

Chunked junior-reference prose; precise terms with plain-language restatement.

## Page / spread density

Sections/labs; sparse but real diagrams.

## Visual complexity

Layered systems with a11y reading order; pattern+label encodings.

## Interaction type

Full elementary cadence including MEASURE and SECURE.

## Concept depth

Representation, transmission, routing/forwarding, interpretation; threat-aware but age-appropriate security; AI as pattern systems with limits — no omniscient AI character.

## Safety constraints

Privacy, fairness, deepfake/misinfo literacy starters; no unnecessary telemetry.

## Digital / media constraints

Junior reference + labs; HTML/PDF caregiver/educator notes.

## Assessment philosophy

Lab notebooks, explainers, peer teach-back; honest uncertainty valued.

## Portfolio / artifact expectations

Required age-appropriate artifacts: diagrams, experiments, short write-ups.
