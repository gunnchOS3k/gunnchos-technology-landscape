# KIDS-ELEM1 Age-Band Architecture

**Band ID:** `KIDS-ELEM1`  
**Guide ages:** Kindergarten–Grade 2 (guide, not deadline)  
**Status:** `DRAFT_INTERNAL`

## Learner profile

Early readers/writers with wide variance. Can handle cause chains, fair use of shared devices, and beginner unplugged CS ideas.

## Caregiver role

Coach and safety partner; school educators may lead.

## Language complexity

40–120 words/spread or short chapters; glossary sidebars.

## Page / spread density

Illustrated reader; 3–5 labeled elements.

## Visual complexity

Contextual scenes; beginner block diagrams with reading order.

## Interaction type

OBSERVE→PREDICT→TEST→EXPLAIN→BUILD→SECURE→REFLECT→TEACH.

## Concept depth

Computers connect in networks; programs are precise instructions; data is information we choose to share or keep.

## Safety constraints

Password hygiene basics; no stranger contact; consent for photos.

## Digital / media constraints

Illustrated reader + activity pack; reflowable preferred when text-heavy.

## Assessment philosophy

Formative try-its; portfolio artifacts over high-stakes tests in-product.

## Portfolio / artifact expectations

Unplugged algorithms, paper networks, reflection sentences.
