# KIDS-PRESCHOOL Age-Band Architecture

**Band ID:** `KIDS-PRESCHOOL`  
**Guide ages:** 3–4 years (guide, not deadline)  
**Status:** `DRAFT_INTERNAL`

## Learner profile

Story-ready, participatory, forming parasocial bonds with stable characters. Can follow simple multi-step play.

## Caregiver role

Still central; begins light teach-back ('you tell me').

## Language complexity

15–40 words/spread; simple why/how; question + wait.

## Page / spread density

2–3 focals; clear story arc per unit.

## Visual complexity

Simple scenes; bold characters; limited palette roles.

## Interaction type

STORY→NOTICE→NAME→TRY→TEACH (age-adapted). Participatory prompts.

## Concept depth

Devices can send messages; instructions are steps; fair/kind sharing of tools.

## Safety constraints

No dark patterns if digital; offline play after each unit.

## Digital / media constraints

Picture/activity + optional audio; co-use default.

## Assessment philosophy

Process praise; no standardized scoring in-product.

## Portfolio / artifact expectations

Drawing of 'my button' / 'my message' kept locally.
