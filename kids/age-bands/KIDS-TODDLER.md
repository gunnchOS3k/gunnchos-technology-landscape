# KIDS-TODDLER Age-Band Architecture

**Band ID:** `KIDS-TODDLER`  
**Guide ages:** 18–36 months (guide, not deadline)  
**Status:** `DRAFT_INTERNAL`

## Learner profile

Mobile, language-bursting, wants to do-it-again. Still limited transfer from video alone; loves repetition and pointing games.

## Caregiver role

Co-reader and play partner. Connect book objects to home devices safely.

## Language complexity

5–20 words/spread; concrete nouns/verbs; home-language welcome.

## Page / spread density

1–2 focals; simple scene.

## Visual complexity

High contrast; minimal clutter; 1–2 characters.

## Interaction type

LOOK/POINT/NAME plus simple TRY with real buttons/switches under supervision.

## Concept depth

Messages move; buttons make things happen; 'same again' loops as precursor to instructions.

## Safety constraints

Supervise all device touch. No stranger chat. Print-first.

## Digital / media constraints

Optional co-use only; autoplay off; short sessions.

## Assessment philosophy

No quizzes. Observe engagement without scoring the child.

## Portfolio / artifact expectations

Photo of a built block 'machine' optional — caregiver-held, not uploaded by default.
