# Commodity analogy card

Use this card when you do **not** have Device Quartet hardware (the expected case).

| Quartet research form factor | Learning role | Commodity analogy examples (not product claims) |
|---|---|---|
| Student 14.5-inch | Sustained desk compute | Clamshell laptop or desktop you already use at a desk |
| Handheld Hybrid | Mobile and docked compute | Phone/tablet alone vs same device with keyboard/dock |
| DS-XL Coder | Learn-to-build local workflow | Larger display + local editor/terminal for build-test cycles |
| Edge IO Wearables | Embodied sensing / body-proximate I/O | Fitness band, earbuds with sensors, or *imagined* body-proximate constraints—without capturing others’ biometrics |

**Rules**

- Analogies teach constraint contrast; they do not prove Quartet EVT numbers.
- Prefer public or synthetic tasks.
- Mark every Quartet physical attribute `PHYSICAL_PENDING`.
