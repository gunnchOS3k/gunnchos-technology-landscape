# Boot symptom card (illustrative fixture — not a real device log)

**Scenario A — cold boot (illustrative).** Power → vendor logo ~3–8 s wall-clock band → lock screen. No recovery text.

**Scenario B — wake (illustrative).** Sleep → brief logo or blank → lock screen faster than cold boot (qualitative only).

**Scenario C — update banner (illustrative).** Lock screen visible with “Update available” text. Does **not** prove interrupted update or secure-boot failure.

Truth class: **ILLUSTRATIVE_FIXTURE**. Do not treat timings as product specs or Quartet EVT.
