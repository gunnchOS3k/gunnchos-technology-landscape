## Safety first

- **No mains work.** Do not open wall-powered equipment or cut power cords.
- **No battery abuse.** Do not short, crush, heat, or puncture batteries.
- **No unauthorized RF.** Do not transmit on frequencies you are not allowed to use.
- **Prefer Route A** (simulation / offline fixture). Optional kits only when supervised and documented as low-voltage educational use.
