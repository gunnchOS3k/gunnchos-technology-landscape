# LAB-BUS-001 scenario card

**Scenario A (preferred):** Personally owned phone or laptop + undamaged commodity cable (charge, data, or both). Visual inspection and ordinary plug/unplug only.

**Scenario B (offline):** No hardware. Use `commodity_path_card.md` and complete the worksheet as a labeled conceptual map of a familiar interconnect you have used before.

**Stop conditions:** Any step that needs opening a device, forcing a fit, electrical probing, or heating/abusing a battery.
