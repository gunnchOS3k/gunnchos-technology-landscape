# Teach-back — LAB-PKT-001

Explain to a nontechnical person, then introduce terms carefully:

1. Your phone or laptop is not “the Internet.”
2. Wi-Fi and cellular are different **ways of attaching** to a path; neither is the whole Internet.
3. The app’s servers might be nearby (**edge**) or farther away (**cloud**)—you usually **infer** that unless you have extra evidence.
4. Feeling stuck can mean long waits (**latency**), errors/retries (**reliability**), or a big slow download (**throughput**)—these are not the same problem.

Avoid claiming RF root cause from signal bars alone.
