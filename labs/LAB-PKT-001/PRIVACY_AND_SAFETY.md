# LAB-PKT-001 — Privacy and safety

## Privacy

- Timing tables store action labels and phase names—not payload content.
- Redact emails, account names, precise locations, and message previews from screenshots.
- Cellular vs Wi-Fi choice can imply location patterns; record access mode as a category, not a GPS trail.
- Prefer fixtures or public documentation URLs over production accounts.

## Safety

- Do not capture other users’ traffic.
- No unauthorized scanning; no packet capture on networks you do not administer (loopback/demo/fixture only unless policy allows).
- No passwords, session tokens, message bodies, banking amounts, or classmate personal data in artifacts.
- No radio transmission experiments; no captive-portal term bypass; no evil-twin or cracking tutorials.
- Public Wi-Fi classrooms: prefer Route B.
- Physical safety: do not climb for signal; avoid distracted walking-and-testing.
