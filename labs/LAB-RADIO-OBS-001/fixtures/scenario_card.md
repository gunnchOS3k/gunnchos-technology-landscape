# LAB-RADIO-OBS-001 scenario card

**Scenario.** A short video or sync works at a desk, stalls in another room or kitchen-adjacent seat, then improves near a window—while the connection icon often stays lit.

**Learner task.** Record observations only; diagram human → device/antenna → obstacles → access node → beyond; refuse invented RF numbers.

**Fixture truth class.** Illustrative if no live path is used.
